Keyword Research
Our seed keywords include “housing disrepair”, “tenant rights”, “landlord repairs”, “damp mold compensation”, etc. Using industry tools (SEMrush/Ahrefs), we identified core and long-tail terms (Table 2). All volumes are UK-average; CPCs reflect high-value legal terms (often £1–£2+). Difficulty (KD) is generally high for head terms, medium for niche phrases. Search intent is mostly transactional/informational (tenants seeking claims information).

Searcher Intent: Most queries indicate users seeking to claim (transactional), while others seek information (e.g. rights, legal definitions). We classify accordingly to tailor content and landing pages.

SERP Ranking Factors
On-Page: Success requires authoritative, well-structured content. Pages should feature primary keywords in titles/headers, concise yet comprehensive answers, FAQs, and clear CTAs. For example, Satchell Moran’s page succinctly frames “Housing Disrepair Claims Solicitors” and addresses the tenant’s concerns. Content must cite statutes (e.g. Homes (Fitness for Human Habitation) Act 2018, Awaab’s Law) and reputable guidance (Shelter) to build trust and relevance.

Technical: The site will use Next.js deployed on Cloudflare Pages (static export with an `out/` directory). We will pre-render all pages (SSG) so crawlers get full HTML. Performance (Core Web Vitals) is critical; we optimize images (Next/Image unoptimized on export), compress assets, and use Cloudflare's CDN caching plus `_headers`/`_redirects` for edge control. Structured data (e.g. LegalService, LocalBusiness, FAQ schema) is added for rich results.

E‑A‑T and Trust: Law is YMYL; Google demands high Expertise/Authority/Trust. Our site will feature attorney bios/credentials, link to legal resources, and encourage reviews. For example, Osborne’s Law cites Chambers/Legal500 explicitly, a good trust signal. We will similarly display accreditations, partner logos (ADR, Consumer Code) and case results (anonymized).

Backlinks and Authority: Links from credible sources (council sites, law directories, news media) are key. Competitors like Anthony Gold have strong profiles (8,378 links from 4,357 domains). We will seek partnerships (e.g. citizen advice, legal blogs) and PR to earn links. Anchor text should include relevant phrases (“housing disrepair claim”, etc.) but avoid exact-match abuse.

Local SEO: With offices likely in specific UK regions (e.g. London, Manchester), we’ll optimize Google Business profiles and local citations. Local SEO is critical for solicitors. Strategy includes geo-targeted content (e.g. “London housing disrepair lawyer”), including city names in titles/meta, and schema markup for each location. Encouraging local reviews on Google/Trustpilot will boost local rankings.
