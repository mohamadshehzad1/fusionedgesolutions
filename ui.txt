Content Strategy & Site Architecture
A clear silo (pillar–cluster) structure is recommended. Pillar pages target broad themes with clusters of related pages:

Pillar: Housing Disrepair Claims (Main Page)
Covers introduction, scope of claims, and links to subtopics. Optimized for “housing disrepair claims”.
Clusters:
Types of Disrepair: e.g. Damp & Mould, Pest Infestations, Structural Faults, Heating/Electrics. Each cluster can cite Shelter (damp/mould cases).
Legal Rights and Laws: The Homes (Fitness…) Act, Section 11 LTA, Statutory Nuisance, Awaab’s Law. A page could cite Shelter’s summary of the 2018 Act.
Claims Process: Step-by-step guides (pre-action protocol, examples from Shelter’s advice). Include an FAQ (schema-marked) on common questions (e.g. “Can I claim for mould?”, “How long does it take?”).
Calculators & Case Examples: A compensation estimator tool and anonymized case studies/success stories.
FAQ and Blog: Regular articles on news (e.g. references to Coroner’s Awaab’s Law findings), Shelter/Gov updates, and more specific queries (e.g. “Can I withhold rent?” which Shelter cautions against).
